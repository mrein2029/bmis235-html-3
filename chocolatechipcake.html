Background.

Chocolate Chip cake recipe was created by my grandma on my dads side. She and a bunch of my other relatives have complied a bunch of different recipes from different family members and created this recipe book. Chocolate Chip cake is made by my grandma for my dads birthday every year. He requests it to be made with large chocolate chips and not the mini ones. It is also served with vanilla ice cream.
